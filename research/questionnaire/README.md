# Questionnaire plan

This folder supports future user research about route choice behavior in Bishkek.

## Example question blocks

- Which route do you prefer for school or work: fastest or shadier?
- What extra time would you accept for a greener route?
- Which route feels safer in the afternoon?
- Do you change route depending on weather or time of day?

## Privacy constraints

- no address-level data;
- no individual trajectory tracking;
- no personally identifiable information for minors;
- only anonymous behavioral patterns for aggregated analysis.

## Future use

The results will support route prioritisation and city-investment analysis without violating privacy.
