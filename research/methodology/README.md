# Project research notes

## Methodology

The project is rooted in a simple idea: real city walking decisions are influenced by more than distance and travel time.

Our methodology combines:
- network-based pedestrian routing;
- environmental conditions, such as sun exposure and shade;
- urban greenery and walking infrastructure;
- safety-related quality factors such as sidewalk continuity and crossings;
- multi-objective evaluation rather than single-score optimisation.

## Hypothesis

Comfort-aware routing may bring measurable value to pedestrians in Bishkek when the cost of the route is acceptable, especially during hot or sunny periods.

## Core measurement dimensions

- total walking time
- total distance
- shade ratio
- greenery coverage
- sidewalk quality
- crossing quality
- stairs / vertical barriers
- safety and exposure penalties

## Expected research questions

1. Do pedestrians accept a longer route if it is noticeably shadier or greener?
2. Are users more willing to detour during hot daytime conditions?
3. Does comfort-aware routing improve trust in the map compared with common shortest-path logic?

This directory is intended for methodology documentation and future research notes.
